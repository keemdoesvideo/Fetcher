updates

what's changed in fetcher, newest first.

v0.2 latest 22 aug 2026
  • real youtube fetching — paste a link and get an mp4 video or an mp3 audio file, at the quality and filename you choose
  • a live progress bar you can cancel mid-fetch, with clear, friendly messages when something goes wrong
  • fetcher now runs as a small local app — yt-dlp and ffmpeg do the heavy lifting behind the scenes
  • new donate and about pages
v0.1 15 aug 2026
  • the fetch interface — url bar, a video/audio toggle, and the supported-services list
  • settings for video quality, audio quality, and filename style, saved between visits
  • light and dark themes, with a reduced-motion option

on the horizon: support for more sites beyond youtube.
