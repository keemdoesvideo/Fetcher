about

v0.2

fetcher is a small, free tool for saving video and audio from the web. paste a link, choose video or audio, and it lands in your downloads. no ads, no trackers, no paywalls, just a tool that works when you need it.

what it does

fetcher saves video and audio from the sites you already use, including youtube, tiktok, soundcloud, twitch, instagram, and x. paste a link and get a clean mp4 or mp3 at the quality and filename style you choose in settings. if your exact quality isn't available, fetcher quietly steps down to the next best one. more sites are on the way.

open source

fetcher's code is open for anyone to read, learn from, report issues on, or improve.
