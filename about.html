why i built it

fetcher started as something i wanted for myself: paste the link, pick what i want, get the file. that was the whole brief.

the part i cared about just as much was how it felt. little movements, useful settings, no clutter and nothing shouting at you. it should feel more like a tiny app you keep around than a download website you put up with.

what fetcher is

a free, self-built media tool for saving video and audio from the places people already share things. youtube, tiktok, soundcloud, twitch, instagram and x are all supported today, with image and chat tools deliberately waiting in the wings.
