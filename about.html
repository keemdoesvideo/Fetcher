project fetcher

about

v0.2

fetcher is a small, free tool for saving video and audio from the web — paste a link, choose video or audio, and it lands in your downloads.

what it does

right now fetcher handles youtube — normal watch links and shorts — as an mp4 video or an mp3 audio file, at the quality and filename style you pick in settings. if your exact quality isn't available it quietly steps down to the next best. more sites are on the way.

how it works

fetcher runs entirely on your own computer. the heavy lifting is done by two well-known open-source tools, yt-dlp and ffmpeg, wrapped in a small local server. nothing about what you fetch is sent anywhere except the request to the site you're downloading from — there are no accounts, and no tracking.

open source

fetcher's code is open for anyone to read, learn from, report issues on, or improve.
