settings

saved
theme

auto follows your system's light or dark setting. light and dark keep fetcher fixed to that look regardless of your system.

shortcuts button

the floating shortcuts pill in the bottom-right of the fetch page. hide it for a cleaner look.

downloads button

the floating downloads pill in the top-right. it only appears after a download, and clears itself after a week without one.

preferred quality

if your preferred quality isn't available, fetcher will use the next best one.

video format
videos are saved asMP4

mp4 is the only format fetcher supports right now. more are on the way.

audio format
audio is saved asMP3

mp3 is the only format fetcher supports right now. more are on the way.

preferred audio quality

fetcher won't increase quality beyond the original audio source.

filename style

clean gives your file a simple, readable name. original keeps the source's own filename where possible.

motion

system follows your device's reduce-motion setting. reduced minimises non-essential movement. reserved keeps fetcher snappy with little or no overshoot. full keeps fetcher's playful bounce and overshoot.
